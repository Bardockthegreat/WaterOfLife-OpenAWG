# Build Guide

## Step 1 — Frame
Construct a simple wooden or foam-board frame with:
- Back panel for hot-side heatsink
- Middle section for cold duct
- Lower section for funnel + filter + jug

## Step 2 — Hot Side
Mount CPU/GPU heatsink + fan with unobstructed airflow.

## Step 3 — Thermal Stack
Apply thermal paste, clamp Peltier between hot and cold sides, add fins, insulate edges.

## Step 4 — Cold Duct
Seal cold plate + fins inside a duct. Add intake fan (15–30 CFM). Add outlet vent.

## Step 5 — Water Path
Slope surface ≥5°, add drip edge, mount funnel, connect tubing → carbon filter → jug.

## Step 6 — Electrical
12 V PSU → fuse → switch → parallel loads.
Peltiers via buck converter (9–10 V). Fans at 12 V.

# Safety Guidelines

## Electrical Safety
- Use 12 V DC only.
- Inline fuse (15–20 A) required.
- Keep PSU dry and elevated.
- Keep wiring away from condensate.
- Use proper wire gauge (14–16 AWG for Peltiers).

## Thermal Safety
- Hot side may reach 40–70°C.
- Cold side may frost.
- Avoid touching thermal stack during operation.

## Water Safety
- Use food-grade tubing.
- Replace carbon filter regularly.
- Clean funnel and jug frequently.

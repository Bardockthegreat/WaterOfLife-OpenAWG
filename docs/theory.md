# Theory of Operation

This document explains the physics behind the Atmospheric Water Generator (AWG).

## 1. Dew Point and Condensation
Water condenses when a surface is cooled below the dew point of the surrounding air. In 24–30°C and 60–80% RH, dew points range from ~15–26°C.

## 2. Peltier Cooling
TEC1-12706 modules pump heat from the cold side to the hot side. Running them at 9–10 V improves COP and reduces frost.

## 3. Heat Rejection
A CPU/GPU heatsink rated ≥150 W dissipates the combined heat load (Qc + Pin ≈ 80–130 W for two modules).

## 4. Airflow
Slowed airflow (15–30 CFM) increases residence time, allowing the entire airstream to cool below dew point.

## 5. Water Collection
Condensed droplets run down vertical fins, onto a sloped surface, through a funnel, and into a carbon-filtered jug.

This system is simple, low-tech, and physically sound.
